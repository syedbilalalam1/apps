Email sbadevs2@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two days.

Someone was bitten or stung

In an emergency, call 911. For bites, stings and poisonings, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 (US, free, 24/7). Critter Check can't diagnose bites or skin.

Getting a better answer

Fill the frame with the bug, shoot from above in good light, and hold steady. Pick your state in Settings: bugs that don't live there are shown as less likely.

Why "Not sure"?

Some bugs look almost the same in a photo. Rather than guess, Critter Check shows both and the marks that tell them apart. If one of them is dangerous, treat it as that one until you're sure.

Free vs Pro

Safety info and a few checks a day are free. Pro adds names, look-alikes and field marks, the Bug Book and 60 checks a day.

Restore or cancel

Settings → Critter Check Pro → Restore or Manage. You can also cancel in the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Delete my data

Settings → Delete everything. The privacy policy explains what is stored where.
