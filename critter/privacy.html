Privacy Policy

Critter Check: Bug Identifier · Effective September 28, 2026

Critter Check helps you identify common US bugs from a photo. The short version: your Bug Book stays on your iPhone, and the photos you check are used only to answer you and aren't kept.

Photos you check

When you check a bug, the app sends the photo (made smaller) and the US state you picked to our server. The server asks an AI vision model, run by Cloudflare Workers AI, which bugs from our list it shows, and returns the answer. The photo isn't stored on our server and isn't used for advertising or to train AI.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your Bug Book: the finds you save, with their photos and dates.
  • Your state and settings.

Delete it anytime in Settings → Delete everything, or by deleting the app.

Counting free checks

To count free checks, the app sends a random number it created, which isn't linked to you, and the server keeps a daily counter per network address that is deleted automatically. For Pro, the app sends Apple's signed record of your purchase. It has a transaction number, not your name, email or Apple ID.

Reminders

The optional 30-day tick check-in and trial reminders are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone.

Purchases

Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details, name, email or Apple ID.

No tracking, no ads

Critter Check has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. There's no account or sign-in.

Not medical advice

Critter Check is for learning and general safety information. A photo ID can be wrong. It never analyses bites or skin. In an emergency, call 911. For poisonings or bites, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.

Children

Critter Check is suitable for families and doesn't collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
