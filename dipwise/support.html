Which test strips work?

Strips from most brands. Pick your strip type (6-way, 4-way or hot tub) when you test. Dipwise measures each pad's color and suggests the closest chart color. Always check it against the chart on your bottle and tap to change it. Drop kits work too: type the numbers.

My photo reads the wrong color

Test in shade, not direct sun, and read the strip within the time on the bottle. Drag the two dots to the ends of the strip so the circles sit on the pads. You can always tap the right color yourself.

Where do the amounts come from?

From the same formulas Trouble Free Pool's Pool Math uses, with the strength printed on your product. Targets follow the Trouble Free Pool method by default. You can switch to pool store ranges in Settings. Dose one product at a time and retest.

My pool size is a guess

Open Settings, tap your pool, and type the gallons, or use "Work it out from the shape" when you first set up. Doses scale with volume, so the closer the better.

Is this professional advice?

No. Dipwise gives general water-care guidance. Always follow the product label, never mix chemicals, and ask a pool professional for anything unusual.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → Membership → Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Open Settings → Membership → Manage, or the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least a day before it ends.

Delete my data

Open Settings → Delete everything. The privacy policy explains what is stored where.
