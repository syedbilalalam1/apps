Privacy Policy

Dipwise: Pool & Hot Tub Test · Effective September 28, 2026

Dipwise is an independent pool and hot tub water guide. It is not affiliated with any chemical, test strip or pool equipment brand. The short version: everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Dipwise has no account, no server and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your pools and hot tubs: names, sizes and what keeps them clean.
  • Your test results, plans and which steps you ticked off.
  • The products on your shelf and your settings.

This is stored only in the app and protected by your iPhone's passcode. Delete all of it anytime in Settings → Delete everything, or by deleting the app.

Camera and photos

When you test with a photo, the camera takes one picture of your test strip. Dipwise measures the pad colors on your iPhone and doesn't keep or upload the photo.

Reminders

Test-day, hot tub refill and trial reminders are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone, only if you turn them on.

Purchases

Memberships are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details, name, email or Apple ID.

No tracking, no ads

Dipwise has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. There is no account or sign-in, and the app doesn't send your data anywhere.

Chemical safety

Dipwise gives general water-care guidance. Always follow the product label, add one chemical at a time, and never mix chemicals.

Children

Dipwise is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
