Privacy Policy

US Citizenship Test 2026 Ultra · Effective September 23, 2026

US Citizenship Test 2026 Ultra is an independent study app for the U.S. naturalization civics test. It is not affiliated with U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or any government agency. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. The short version: we don't collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell, share or receive your personal information.

What stays on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your iPhone:

  • Your study progress, bookmarks, test results and practice history, including the text of answers you type or say.
  • What you tell the app about yourself, all optional: the first name the app calls you, your birth year (only used to check whether the 65/20 option can apply) and your interview date.
  • Your settings: which test you're taking, the 65/20 option, your state or territory, ZIP code and congressional district, daily goal, voice, speaking speed and reminder time.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase your progress at any time in Profile → Reset all progress, and deleting the app removes everything.

Purchases

The plans go through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, name, email or Apple ID. The app only learns from Apple, on your iPhone, whether you have bought a plan, so it can unlock the app and restore your purchase on a new device. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

Microphone and speech recognition

The practice interview and reading practice ask for microphone and speech recognition access so you can speak out loud. Your speech is converted to text on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere, including to us or to Apple's servers. If your device can't recognize speech on-device, the app asks you to type or check yourself instead. You can turn access off at any time in the Settings app; everything else keeps working.

Updated answers

Some test answers change after elections (for example, the names of the President or your senators). The app downloads a public list of current answers from this website. The download is the same file for everyone and never includes your state, ZIP code or any other information about you. Like any website, our hosting provider receives technical details needed to deliver the file, such as your IP address, and may keep them briefly in server logs under its own policies. We do not use them to identify you.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the naturalization interview and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
