Which test will I take?

If you filed Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025, you take the 2025 civics test (128 questions; up to 20 asked; pass with 12 correct). If you filed earlier, you take the 2008 test (100 questions; up to 10 asked; pass with 6 correct). You can change the version in Profile.

How are my answers checked?

The app compares what you say or type with the official USCIS answers, allowing for small speech-recognition slips, numbers said as words, and extra words like "I think". If you gave a correct answer and it was marked wrong, tap "I said it right" in the mock interview (or "My answer was right" when typing) to count it.

Why does the answer about my senators or representative say "depends on where you live"?

Add your state, and optionally your ZIP code, in Profile → Where you live. Your location stays on your iPhone.

The President, Speaker or my senators changed. How do I update?

Open Profile → Current officials → Check for updated answers. Always confirm current answers at uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview.

The practice interview can't hear me

Allow microphone and speech recognition access in the Settings app → Privacy & Security. Speech is processed on your iPhone only. If your device doesn't support on-device recognition, you can type your answers instead.

How do I start over?

Profile → Reset all progress clears your lessons, XP, streak, saved questions and test history. Your settings and purchases stay.
