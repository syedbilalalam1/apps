Privacy Policy

Dues: Subscription Tracker · Effective September 28, 2026

Dues is an independent subscription tracker. It is not affiliated with any service it lists. The short version: your list stays on your iPhone. Dues has no account, no server and no tracking, and it never connects to your bank or email.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The subscriptions and trials you add: names, prices, dates, notes and reminder choices.
  • Your settings.

This is stored only in the app and protected by your iPhone's passcode. Delete it anytime in Settings → Delete everything, or by deleting the app. You can export it as a CSV file you share yourself.

Reminders

Reminders before charges and trial endings are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone.

Links

"How to cancel" opens the service's own website or Apple's subscriptions page in your browser. Dues sends nothing about you when you tap a link.

Purchases

Dues Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details, name, email or Apple ID.

No tracking, no ads

Dues has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data anywhere.

Children

Dues is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
