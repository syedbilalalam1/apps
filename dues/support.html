Help and Support

Dues: Subscription Tracker

Email sbadevs2@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two days.

I'm not getting reminders

Open the Settings app → Notifications → Dues and make sure notifications are allowed. Then open Dues once; it re-plans every reminder each time it opens. You can change the reminder time in Dues → Settings.

A price is wrong

Prices in the list are typical US list prices. Tap the subscription → Edit and type what you actually pay.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Tap it → "Open cancel page". Anything billed by Apple opens your Apple subscriptions. Dues never logs in or cancels for you. After cancelling, tap "I cancelled it" to add it to your savings.

Free vs Pro

Free covers 2 subscriptions, reminders included. Dues Pro unlocks unlimited subscriptions: pay once for lifetime, or yearly.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → Dues Pro → Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel Dues Pro Yearly

Open Settings → Dues Pro → Manage, or the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Delete my data

Open Settings → Delete everything. The privacy policy explains what is stored where.
