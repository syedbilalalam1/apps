A product isn't found

Tap Snap the ingredients and photograph the ingredients list. The app reads it on your iPhone and gives the full result, even offline.

How is the score worked out?

Every food starts at 100 and every point taken off is shown as its own line, with the reason. In the app, open You → How scores work. Whole foods never lose points for calories or natural fat.

Is this medical advice?

No. The app is for information only. Database entries can be out of date, so always check the package, especially for allergies.

Restore a purchase

Open You → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Open You → Manage subscription, or the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Where does the data come from?

Product data comes from Open Food Facts (Open Database License). Additive facts are written in our own words from the U.S. FDA, the European Food Safety Authority and IARC, and each additive page links to its sources.
