Unprocessed

A tap-through preview of the iPhone app: scan a food, see how processed it is, and follow the math behind every point of its score.

Things to try

  • Swipe the shelf in the camera and tap a pack to scan it.
  • Scan one more pack to finish the kitchen audit on Home.
  • Under a score, tap a cleaner pick, or share the result.
  • Change your weekly goal on the week card and watch the ring.
  • Tap an additive line in Show the math to read its sources.
  • Turn flags on or off under You and watch scores change.
  • Switch Dark, Light or Auto under You.
  • Tap Snap ingredients and paste any real label.

Open the web version to scan with your camera and look up real products. Sample products are made up for the preview. Scores use the app's own rules and its 137-additive database; purchases here are pretend.
