Privacy Policy

Unprocessed: UPF Food Scanner · Effective September 25, 2026

Unprocessed is an independent app that shows how processed a packaged food is and explains its ingredients. It is not affiliated with any food brand, with Open Food Facts or with any government agency. The short version of this policy: we don't collect information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell, share or receive your personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Your scan history and favorites: the products you scanned and their ingredient lists.
  • Your settings, such as which ingredients you asked the app to flag.

This never leaves your iPhone. Delete a scan by swiping it in History; deleting the app removes everything.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, the app sends only that barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to fetch the product's name, ingredients and nutrition. No information about you is included. To suggest cleaner picks, the app also asks Open Food Facts for popular products in the same category (for example “breakfast cereals”), with your country so they're products sold near you. That search contains nothing about you either. Like any website, Open Food Facts receives technical details needed to answer, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy.

Reminders

If you turn on the grocery day reminder, iOS schedules it on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and you can turn it off in the app or in Settings.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read barcodes and ingredient labels. Label photos are read on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition. Photos are never uploaded, stored by us or shared. If you choose a photo from your library, only that photo is read.

Purchases

Plans are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, name, email or Apple ID. The app only learns from Apple, on your iPhone, whether you have a plan.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
