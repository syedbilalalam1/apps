Privacy Policy

Turfday: Lawn Care Planner · Effective September 28, 2026

Turfday is an independent lawn care planner. It is not affiliated with any fertilizer or seed brand. The short version: your lawn and log stay on your iPhone. Turfday has no account, no server and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your town, state and a rounded position of your lawn.
  • Your grass type and lawn size, including sections you measure on the map.
  • Your lawn log (mowing, feeding, seeding, spraying, watering), saved products and settings.

This is stored only in the app and protected by your iPhone's passcode. Delete it anytime in Settings → Delete everything, or by deleting the app.

Location and weather

If you tap "Use my location", Turfday asks iOS for your location once to find your town. You can type a ZIP code instead. To time tasks and watering, Turfday asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for your area's forecast and recent weather. That request goes to Apple under Apple's privacy policy; it isn't sent to us.

Maps

"Measure on the map" shows Apple Maps satellite imagery. The corners you tap are used on your iPhone to work out the area.

Reminders

The optional Monday plan is scheduled by iOS on your iPhone.

Purchases

Turfday Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details, name, email or Apple ID.

No tracking, no ads

Turfday has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data to us.

Children

Turfday is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
