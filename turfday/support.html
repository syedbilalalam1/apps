Email sbadevs2@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two days.

Why does Turfday say to do something this week?

Each task has a window for your grass and where you live, taken from university extension guides (Purdue, Penn State, Texas A&M, UGA). Crabgrass preventer is timed by growing degree days from your local weather. Tap any task to see why and how.

How much fertilizer do I use?

Open the feeding task (or Tools → Fertilizer) and type the three numbers on your bag, like 32-0-4, and the bag weight. Turfday shows the pounds and bags for your lawn. Always check the bag's own label too.

My lawn size is wrong

Open Tools → Measure on the map and tap the corners of each patch of grass. Or change the typed size in Settings.

I picked the wrong grass or place

Open Settings (the gear on This week) to change them. The plan updates right away.

Free vs Pro

This week's tasks and your log are free. Turfday Pro adds the amounts for your lawn, the season ahead, the calculators and the map. Choose yearly (with a 14-day free trial) or pay once for lifetime.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → Turfday Pro → Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel the yearly plan

Open Settings → Turfday Pro → Manage, or the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Cancel during the free trial and you won't be charged.

Delete my data

Open Settings → Delete everything. The privacy policy explains what is stored where.
